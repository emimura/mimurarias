Privacy Policy - APK Privacy Audit

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Summary

APK Privacy Audit inspects the privacy of an APK (an Android app file) and checks how well its Google Play Data safety declaration matches the evidence. The analysis runs entirely on your device: the app never uploads the inspected APK or the results to any server.

The free version shows ads, described below. Ads are served by Google AdMob and may use your device's advertising ID. Beyond ads, the app does not transmit any information about you.

2. Information Processed

The app does not collect personal data and requires no sign-up, login or account. To audit, it reads only what you choose, on the device itself:

  • the APK file you select, or the APKs of installed apps you choose to audit;
  • that APK's manifest and code (DEX), to identify permissions, third-party SDKs, endpoints and exported components.

This data is processed locally and discarded when you end the audit or close the app. No APK, code snippet, manifest text or report leaves the device.

The only information processed by third parties is Google AdMob's, and only in the free version:

  • Advertising ID - serving and measuring ads
  • Ad interaction data - serving, personalizing and measuring ads

We do not collect your name, email, phone, address, contacts, photos, files, health data, location or browsing history. We do not send crash logs, stack traces, app performance data, network requests or diagnostic telemetry. If the app crashes, it is not reported anywhere.

3. How Information Is Used

AdMob information is used solely to serve, measure and limit ads in the free version. We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

4. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads: a banner on the home screen and an app-open ad when you start or return to the app. The Google SDK may collect your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data, under Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policy. Ads may be personalized based on your device and your in-app activity.

The paid version has no ads and does not include the AdMob SDK.

5. Permissions

The free version requests:

  • INTERNET - to load AdMob ads
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - to detect the network type for ads
  • AD_ID and Google Ad Services permissions - to obtain the advertising ID required by AdMob
  • WAKE_LOCK - to keep ad requests working while the app is in the background
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE - required by the Google Play Services ads SDK

To let you pick an installed app and audit its APK, the app queries the list of installed apps (package visibility). It does not access those apps' data beyond the APK you ask it to analyze. The paid version requests neither INTERNET nor any ad permission.

6. Data Security

Because the analysis is local, no audit data is in transit. Ad data is transmitted by Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not store credentials or personal data on our servers.

7. Retention and Deletion

We keep no user database and no server that receives information about who uses the app. Generated reports stay on your device and can be deleted by you at any time. Ad data is retained by the Google AdMob network under their policies.

You can reset your advertising ID, limit tracking on your device, or delete ad data in your device settings.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The app itself does not use cookies. The Google AdMob SDK may use device identifiers for advertising and analytics, under the Google policy linked in section 4.

9. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. We do not serve personalized ads to users under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through an app update or this page.
