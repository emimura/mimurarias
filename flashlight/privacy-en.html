Privacy Policy - Strobe SOS Flashlight

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Summary

Strobe SOS Flashlight is a utility app that uses only your phone's flash. It does not require an account, a login, or any personal information from you.

The flashlight itself works without internet, but the free version shows ads and sends diagnostic data, described below. In other words, the app is not offline in the sense of transmitting nothing.

2. Information Collected

The app does not collect personal information directly. The following information is processed by third parties:

  • Advertising ID - ad display and measurement
  • Ad interaction data - ad display, personalization, and measurement
  • Technical performance data - errors, slowness, crashes, and network requests, handled by New Relic (see section 4.1)
  • IP address - used only to derive approximate server location, as described in section 4.1

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, contacts, photos, files, health information, precise location, or browsing history. None of that information is sent to New Relic or any other third party.

3. Use of Information

The information is used exclusively to:

  • Display, measure, and cap ads
  • Diagnose errors, slowness, and crashes in the app (New Relic)
  • Improve the stability and performance of future versions

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing. We do not use diagnostic data for advertising.

4. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads, as a banner on the main screen and as an app-open ad when you return to the app. Google's SDK may collect your device advertising identifier and ad interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and advertising policies. Ads may be personalized based on your device and your activity in the app.

The paid version of the app contains no ads and no AdMob SDK.

4.1. Performance Monitoring (New Relic)

The app uses the New Relic agent to detect errors, slowness, and crashes. The agent is integrated into the app and sends performance data only, over the internet, with encryption (HTTPS) and server certificate validation. Its handling is described in the New Relic Privacy Policy.

On your device, the app only calls the agent's start method with the application token. We do not send a user ID, email, name, or any custom attribute: there is no code in the app that ties telemetry to you.

What is sent to New Relic:

  • About the device: model, manufacturer, OS version, carrier name, screen size and orientation, session duration, and a unique installation identifier generated by the agent and stored only in the app's own preferences
  • About network requests: the address of requests made by the app (in this case, AdMob ad calls), with HTTP status code, response time, and response size. The agent strips the query string, fragment, username, and password from the URL before sending it
  • On error: the first 2 kilobytes of the response body and the call stack trace, only when the HTTP response is an error (4xx or 5xx) or the connection fails
  • On crash: the error stack trace and thread state, so the cause can be identified
  • IP address: used only as input to derive country, state, postal code, and approximate coordinates. New Relic states that it does not retain the IP after this conversion and keeps it in memory for at most six hours

This data is used to diagnose failures and slowness, and not for advertising or marketing. Performance data stays only in the app's memory while it is open, and is not written to the device's storage.

If you would rather not send diagnostic telemetry, uninstall the app. There is no in-app switch to disable the agent.

5. Permissions

The app requests these permissions:

  • FLASHLIGHT - to turn on the device flash
  • INTERNET - to load ads and send the diagnostic data described in section 4.1
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - to identify the network type in order to serve ads and send telemetry
  • AD_ID and Google Ad Services permissions - to obtain the advertising identifier, required by AdMob
  • WAKE_LOCK - to keep telemetry delivery running while the app is in the background
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE - to allow performance data collection in the background

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other personal access. The flashlight is controlled directly through the flash hardware, without opening the camera.

6. Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and follows industry-standard security practices. We do not store credentials or personal data on our servers.

7. Retention and Deletion

We do not keep a database of user data. Ad data is retained by the respective advertisers and by Google's AdMob network according to their policies. Performance data is handled by New Relic according to its privacy policy and services privacy notice.

You can reset your Advertising ID, limit tracking at the device level, or delete ad data in your device settings. To request deletion of diagnostic data associated with this app, contact us at the email address in section 11.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The app itself does not use cookies. Google's AdMob SDK may use device identifiers for advertising and analytics, as described in Google's policy linked in section 4. The New Relic agent stores an installation identifier it generates in the app's preferences, and does not create cookies.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children or adolescents under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. We do not allow personalized ads to users under 13.
